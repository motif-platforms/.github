# Motif Platforms

**AI + Data = Better Decisions.** We are an AI consultancy that works from strategy to production.

We help organizations move AI from slideware to working systems. That means strategy, transformation management and growth, backed by engineers who build and evaluate the systems themselves.

### What we build
- **Agent evaluation:** harnesses, graders and benchmarks for knowledge-work AI
- **Decision platforms:** internal command centers and reporting that put data in front of the people deciding
- **Production web:** content-driven sites on Next.js, Sanity, Supabase and Vercel

### Why most repos are private
Client engagements and internal tooling stay private by design. The open-source projects below are clean, standalone versions of the patterns we use.
